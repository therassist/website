Therassist is not a system of record Therassist has no concept of a patient chart. There is no patient record to create, maintain, or reconcile against your EHR, and no patient-identifiable data is held once a session is finalized. Notes are drafted for the clinician to review and then moved into your own record system. The answers below describe what happens to session data along the way.
1

Recordings

Are recordings ever used or accessed by the AI?
No. Only de-identified transcripts are used by our AI quality and adherence tools.
What happens to session recordings?

That depends on how you record. Therassist supports two recording paths, handled differently.


Zoom Recording If you record through Zoom, recordings are managed according to your own Zoom account configuration. Therassist does not control Zoom recordings. We recommend deleting recordings within 24 hours of the session and never more than 72 hours. Some practices retain Zoom recordings longer for legitimate clinical purposes — such as video review in supervision — and that is your practice's decision to make within your own HIPAA policies.
In-App Recording If you record using Therassist's built-in record-and-transcribe feature, the audio recording is deleted automatically and immediately as soon as transcription is complete, which is typically within 90 seconds of the end of a session. No recording is retained.
What about recordings submitted for expert coding and validation purposes?
Recordings submitted as part of the project to validate our AI quality and adherence tools are held in encrypted storage only for the processing window the expert human rating requires — typically within seven days, and in no event longer than fourteen. Once the rating is complete, the recording is permanently deleted.
2

Transcripts and De-identification

Are session transcripts stored after a session?

For all sessions, transcripts are de-identified immediately upon ingestion, before anything is written to the database or sent to any AI model. Patient identifiers are removed by an automated pipeline, and the original transcript is deleted. The pipeline is designed to fail closed: if the de-identification service is unavailable, ingestion fails rather than allowing identified data through.


Our de-identification methodology has been reviewed by an independent HIPAA expert under the Expert Determination method of 45 CFR 164.514(b)(1), who determined that it meets the very small risk standard the regulation requires.


For routine sessions, de-identified transcripts are deleted when the clinician generates a note or scorecard and deletes the session, or within 72 hours — whichever occurs first.


For validation sessions, de-identified transcripts are used to help improve our AI quality and adherence tools. This data is kept in accordance with research standards for data handling and retention.

Can Therassist staff read my clients' session transcripts?

For routine sessions, transcript processing is automated. Transcripts are not accessible to Therassist staff in readable form. Accessing them would require a deliberate unauthorized action, which would trigger an immediate alert and be captured in our audit logs.


We hold our subcontractors to the same standard. Contractually, they are prohibited from using session data for any purpose beyond delivering their specific service. Upon ingestion of a transcript, we programmatically instruct subcontractors to delete both the recording and the transcript immediately, which they attest to contractually. No subcontractors retain session content beyond what is technically necessary to complete their specific task.


Validation sessions are handled differently. Because the session data are used to improve the accuracy of our AI quality and adherence tools, de-identified transcripts from validation sessions are reviewed by members of our quality team of expert raters for that specific purpose. This access is limited, purposeful, and covered explicitly in the patient consent form that practices obtain before submitting any validation recordings.

How is the de-identified transcript from a validation session used?
De-identified validation transcripts are used by our quality team to refine the AI's ability to recognize and accurately rate therapeutic adherence and quality, and to provide feedback to therapists. This work is oriented entirely toward improving our AI quality and adherence tools. It is not used for any other purpose.
What happens to data from a routine session?

Routine sessions are any session not submitted for validation. Here is how data flows through each step:


  1. Recording: Sessions are recorded either through Zoom or the in-app record-and-transcribe feature. Both work for in-person and telehealth sessions.
  2. Transcription: With Zoom, a transcript is created in Zoom and ingested by Therassist. The Zoom recording itself is never ingested — it stays in your practice's Zoom account and is deleted according to your Zoom configuration (we recommend within 24 hours). With the in-app feature, the audio recording is deleted automatically as soon as the transcript is created.
  3. De-identification: Transcripts are de-identified immediately upon ingestion, under a methodology reviewed by an independent HIPAA expert under 45 CFR 164.514(b)(1). The original transcript is deleted.
  4. Note and scorecard generation: The de-identified transcript is used to generate a session note and/or a quality and adherence scorecard. Recordings are never input into the AI — only de-identified transcripts are used.
  5. Deletion: The de-identified transcript is deleted when the clinician finalizes and deletes the session, or within 72 hours, whichever comes first. Once it is deleted, Therassist holds no data from the session that could identify a patient. Scorecards persist — they contain no patient identifiers, are associated with the clinician rather than the patient, and are retained so that progress can be tracked over time.

What happens to data from a validation session?

Validation sessions are sessions a clinician chooses to submit for expert quality and adherence rating. The process differs from routine sessions in how the recording is handled:


  1. Recording and upload: The clinician uses the in-app record-and-transcribe feature to create an audio recording, or uploads a video recording from their computer, and selects the option to submit for validation.
  2. Secure storage: The recording is moved to a secure, HIPAA-compliant storage area accessible only to the expert raters on our quality team.
  3. Expert rating: A human expert rates the session using validated quality and adherence measures — typically within seven days, and in no event longer than fourteen. As soon as the rating is complete, the recording is permanently deleted.
  4. Quality and adherence feedback report: The feedback report is sent automatically to the clinician by email. It is up to the practice whether to disassociate the clinician's identity from the rating.
  5. Data retention: The expert rating, de-identified transcript, and AI quality and adherence rating are used to validate and improve our AI quality and adherence tools. This data is retained in accordance with research data standards.

How Data Is Used

Does Therassist use session data to improve its AI?
For routine sessions, we do not use session data to improve our AI. For validation sessions, Therassist uses de-identified transcripts to help improve the performance of our AI quality and adherence tools — and for no other purpose.
Does my clients' data go to OpenAI, Anthropic, or other AI labs?

Session data does not go to foundation model laboratories for training or retention. When Therassist scores a session, de-identified transcript content is sent to AI infrastructure (e.g., OpenAI's GPT models, Anthropic's Claude models) accessed through cloud providers (e.g., Microsoft Azure, AWS) — both operating under Business Associate Agreements — for processing. Those providers are contractually prohibited from retaining session content or using it to train their models.


The key distinction Therassist uses AI to process your data. It does not send your data back to train the AI. Those are different things, and we have designed our systems and contracts specifically to maintain that separation.
What is the data ever used for?
De-identified data from validation sessions, retained by Therassist, is used only for the purpose of improving our AI quality and adherence tools.
Is Therassist HIPAA-compliant?
Yes. Therassist operates under a Business Associate Agreement with each practice. Every subprocessor who may access protected health information also operates under a BAA. Session content is encrypted in transit and at rest.
What security measures protect session data?

Therassist follows security protocols designed to protect sensitive clinical data at every layer. Session content is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access to data is strictly controlled — only personnel with a specific, documented need can access it, and all subcontractors who handle protected health information operate under Business Associate Agreements. We monitor for unauthorized access and maintain audit logs.


Formal third-party security certification is on our roadmap. In the meantime, we are happy to provide our current security documentation to practices whose compliance requirements need it — contact us at support@therassist.ai.
